Fleet & Dispatch Simulation

Pause-Pay: Dispatch Interceptor & Heat Protection

Role: Backend & Simulation Developer Stack: Java, Spring Boot, AWS SDK, Simulation Engine Repo: DynM13/Pause-Pay

On-demand delivery platforms assign orders primarily based on proximity, ignoring cumulative driver heat strain during high-temperature conditions. Because couriers are paid strictly per delivery, taking unpaid cooling breaks directly reduces earnings.

Pause-Pay models an alternative dispatch workflow: tracking cumulative heat exposure per courier, pausing order assignments when safe limits are reached, and calculating micro-wage credits during rest periods to prevent income loss.

Technical Highlights

  • Cumulative Exposure Tracking: Calculates thermal dose across shift duration rather than relying on a static ambient temperature point.
  • Dispatch Intervention: Intercepts closest-courier assignment when a driver hits exposure thresholds, redirecting new orders to available lower-strain drivers.
  • Recovery Routing: Directs couriers taking cooling breaks to designated shaded rest zones and hydration points.
  • Rest Wage Credits: Models a credit calculation system that offsets lost earnings during mandatory cooling intervals.
  • Shift Simulator: Replays identical shift delivery traffic through standard nearest-driver dispatch versus Pause-Pay dispatch to compare completion rates and driver exposure profiles.
  • AWS Credential Isolation: Configured server-side AWS SDK clients via the default credential provider chain, keeping cloud keys off the frontend.

Research — IITM

Tropospheric Temperature Gradient (TTG) & El Niño Wind Animation

Role: Research Intern & Visualization Developer Stack: Python, Matplotlib, NumPy, JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas Institution: Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM)

The Tropospheric Temperature Gradient (TTG) measures upper-air thermal contrast between South Asia and the equatorial Indian Ocean—a primary thermodynamic driver of the Indian Summer Monsoon. During El Niño events, anomalous equatorial Pacific warming alters Walker circulation, reducing TTG and weakening low-level southwesterly monsoon winds over India.

Technical Highlights

  • Wind Vector Processing: Parsed gridded reanalysis wind datasets ($u$ and $v$ velocity vectors at 850 hPa and 200 hPa) in Python, computing wind speed, divergence, and direction angles.
  • Matplotlib Modeling: Generated baseline streamplots and quiver visualizations in Python using matplotlib to evaluate circulation anomalies during normal versus El Niño years.
  • Frontend Animation: Exported coordinate vector flows to an interactive in-browser canvas animation hosted live on the Ocean Society of India portal.

The research methodology and animation were published in Ocean Digest (June 2026 issue) ↗ by the Ocean Society of India.

Data Structures

Custom LRU Cache

Role: Backend & Algorithms Developer Stack: Java, Doubly Linked List, HashMap, Concurrency Repo: DynM13/custom-LRU-cache

A low-latency, fixed-capacity in-memory cache in Java providing strictly $O(1)$ time complexity for lookup and eviction using an automated Least Recently Used (LRU) policy.

Technical Highlights

  • Strict O(1) Time Complexity: Combined a bidirectional Doubly Linked List with a Java HashMap to ensure constant-time $O(1)$ reads (get) and inserts (put).
  • Direct Pointer Management: Handled node detachment, head insertion (most recently accessed), and tail removal (least recently accessed) explicitly without third-party collection wrappers.
  • Automated Eviction: Automatically evicts the least recently accessed tail node from both the linked list and map when maximum capacity is reached on insertion.
  • Concurrency Controls: Incorporated synchronization blocks to safeguard pointer consistency across concurrent multithreaded read/write operations.
